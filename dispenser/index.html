💧 Water Dispenser Care

For the VECYS water and ice machine. Do the first clean once, then just follow the reminders.

Do once, before using it

🧽 First clean

It's used, so clean it before you drink from it. About 1 hour. Tap each step when it's done.

  • Unplug it. Take off the water bottle.
  • Drain old water. Put a bucket or bowl under the cap on the back and open it. Close it when empty.
  • Wash the loose parts (ice basket, scoop, drip tray) in the sink with dish soap and warm water.
  • Mix bleach water: 1 gallon of water + 1 tablespoon of plain bleach. Put it in where the water normally goes.
  • Plug in and wait 5 minutes. Then press every tap (cold, room, hot) until it runs out.
  • Rinse 4 times. Fill with fresh water and run it out of every tap, 4 times. No bleach smell = done.
  • Run self-clean. Fill with fresh water, hold the ice button for 3 seconds. It cleans itself for about 10 minutes. Drain at the back.
  • Throw out the first 2–3 batches of ice. Then it's ready.
Every week

🧊 Empty the ice bin

5 minutes. The ice bin is not a freezer, so old ice melts and sits.

  1. Dump any old ice.
  2. Wipe the ice bin and scoop with soapy water, then dry.
  3. Empty and wipe the drip tray.

Every month

🍶 Vinegar clean

About 30 minutes. Stops white crust and keeps the ice tasting clean.

  1. Mix half white vinegar, half water and put it in where the water goes.
  2. Hold the ice button for 3 seconds to start self-clean (about 10 minutes).
  3. Drain at the back.
  4. Fill with plain water and run self-clean 2 more times to wash out the vinegar.

Every 3 months

✨ Deep clean

About 1 hour. On these days, skip that month's vinegar clean, it's included.

  1. Do steps 1 to 6 of the First clean above (bleach, then rinse 4 times).
  2. Then do the Vinegar clean.

⚠️ Never

  • Never mix bleach and vinegar. Together they make a toxic gas. Rinse fully between them.
  • No steel wool, hard brushes, or rubbing alcohol on it. They scratch and damage it.
  • Always unplug before cleaning.
  • Going away for a week or more? Drain it first.
